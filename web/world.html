Pandora

Avatar

Appearance is a component. Changing it submits a command, which lands in the event log like everything else.

This place remembers

A region exists as a formula until somebody witnesses it. From then on it keeps a log — and the log, not the state, is what is stored.

Selection

Everything in the world is a slot with components — an imported avatar's forearm and a box you spawned are the same kind of thing.

Renderer

Primary rays march the same height field the simulation walks on. Shadows, water reflection, and ambient occlusion are secondary rays.

World

A checkpoint captures entities, fields, the semantic graph, and region memory. Restoring one puts the world — and everything it remembers — back exactly.

Nothing here exists
until you look at it.

Every ridge, plant, and drifting seed is a pure function of one seed and a coordinate — no terrain file, no scene graph, no meshes. The moment you witness a region it starts keeping a history: your footfalls stay lit, the plants remember the first time you passed, and anything you take stays taken.
